# Sealcard — Play listing (EN)

## App name
Sealcard

## Short description
Seal a photo. Keep it in a series binder.

## Full description
Shoot. Seal. File it in a binder.

Sealcard turns a photo into a sealed card and keeps it in a series binder. Once sealed, a card cannot be edited. What you kept stays as it was.

• Capture with the camera and seal immediately
• Frame templates and date stamps
• Organize series in binders
• Exhibit cards inside the app
• Share as an image (system share sheet)
• Save an image for wallpaper

Everything stays on your device. No account, no cloud, no ads.

Permissions
• Camera: used to take photos
• Photos write: only when you choose to save for wallpaper
